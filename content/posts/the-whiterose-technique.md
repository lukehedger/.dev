> Whiterose is a Chinese transgender woman with an obsession for managing xyr time.

— [http://mrrobot.wikia.com/wiki/Whiterose](http://mrrobot.wikia.com/wiki/Whiterose)

Here’s how to run your meetings like Whiterose for **maximum production**:

1.  Allocate time for a meeting based on importance, where 1 is least important:

-   1: 5 mins
-   2: 10 mins
-   3: 15 mins
-   \+ 5 mins per person to ‘have their say’

2\. Start timer with single beep alert every minute. Feel the time-pressure!

3\. If the meeting should overrun, note what still needs to be discussed and consider if this constitutes a 2nd meeting.

4\. Non-Whiterose meetings should be kept to a minimum (no more than once a week).
